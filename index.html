Deep Night Tales Motor

Private production tool of the YouTube channel Deep Night Tales

Deep Night Tales Motor is a small, private tool that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It helps produce and schedule the channel's horror narration videos.

The tool connects to YouTube in read-only mode, with the channel owner's own Google account, to do two things: find the link of an episode that the owner has already uploaded, and read the channel's own statistics.

It is not a public service. Nobody other than the channel owner signs in to it, and it cannot upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube.
